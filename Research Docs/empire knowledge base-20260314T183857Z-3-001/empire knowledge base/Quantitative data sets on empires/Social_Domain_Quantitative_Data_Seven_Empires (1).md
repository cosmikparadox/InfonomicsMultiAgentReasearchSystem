## Executive Summary

This comprehensive data compilation reveals striking variation in social mobility mechanisms across pre-modern empires, with the Tang examination system achieving **1-2% pass rates** among approximately 2,000 annual candidates, the Ottoman devşirme system processing **200,000-300,000 boys** over three centuries, and Venice maintaining an essentially closed patriciate of **200-250 families** for 500 years. Gini coefficient estimates exist for only four of the seven target empires, ranging from **0.39-0.44 for Rome** to **0.70-0.76 for Venice**, with the Abbasid Caliphate and Mongol Empire lacking any scholarly inequality estimates due to insufficient source materials.

## I. ROMAN EMPIRE (27 BCE – 476 CE)

### A. Elite Composition and Senatorial Class Evolution

The Roman Senate maintained remarkable numerical stability under the Empire following Augustus's reforms. The body standardized at approximately **600 members** after Augustus reduced it from Julius Caesar's inflated 900. The senatorial order required a property qualification of **1,000,000 sesterces**, necessarily held in Italian land, establishing a wealth threshold roughly **1,111 times** a legionary's annual pay of 900 sesterces [HIGH confidence].

The equestrian order formed a substantially larger secondary elite, with estimates ranging to approximately **30,000 men** across the empire at its peak in the second century CE. Equestrian status required **400,000 sesterces** in property [HIGH confidence].

| Period | Senate Size | Notes | Confidence |
|--------|------------|-------|------------|
| Republic (post-Kings) | 300 | Standard Republican size | HIGH |
| Sulla's reforms (80s BCE) | 600 | Doubled through equestrian inclusion | HIGH |
| Julius Caesar | 900 | Dramatic expansion | HIGH |
| Augustus onward | 600 | Stabilized | HIGH |
| Late Empire (4th century) | 4,000+ | Combined Eastern/Western | MODERATE |

### B. Citizenship Expansion

The Augustan censuses recorded **4,063,000 citizens** in 28 BCE, rising to **4,937,000** by 14 CE. The *Constitutio Antoniniana* of 212 CE extended citizenship to virtually all free inhabitants, transforming citizenship from a privilege held by perhaps **4-7% of the empire's population** under Augustus to a universal status for free persons [HIGH confidence].

Slave population estimates: **10-20% of total population**, with Roman Italy containing **1-1.5 million enslaved persons** representing **13-28% of the Italian population** [MODERATE confidence].

### C. Gini Coefficient Estimates

| Metric | Value | Period | Source | Confidence |
|--------|-------|--------|--------|------------|
| Gini coefficient | 0.39 | 14 CE | Milanovic et al. 2007 | MODERATE |
| Gini coefficient | 0.41-0.44 | 150 CE | Scheidel & Friesen 2009 | MODERATE |
| Top 1% income share | 16.1% | 14 CE | Milanovic et al. 2007 | MODERATE |
| Extraction ratio | 75-94% | Imperial period | Milanovic et al. 2007 | MODERATE |

### D. Social Mobility Pathways

Roman manumission practices were notably frequent. Freedmen faced legal bars from holding office themselves but their children possessed full legal rights, enabling second-generation advancement to senatorial status. The cursus honorum established formalized career progression with approximately **20 quaestorships** filled annually [HIGH confidence].

## II. BYZANTINE EMPIRE (330-1453 CE)

### A. Prosopographical Database Coverage

- **PBW (Prosopography of the Byzantine World, 1025-1180)**: ~10,000 individuals with ~60,000 factoids
- **PBE**: 641-867 CE
- **PmbZ**: 641-1025 CE (5 volumes)
- **Prosopographisches Lexikon der Palaiologenzeit**: 1261-1453

The Kletorologion of Philotheos (899 CE) documents **60 higher ranks** in court hierarchy, with **50 of these 60 positions available to eunuchs** [HIGH confidence].

### B. Eunuch Price Differentials (Social Mobility Indicator)

| Category | Price (nomismata) | Confidence |
|----------|------------------|------------|
| 10-year-old child | 10 | MODERATE |
| 10-year-old castrated | 30 | MODERATE |
| Adult male | 20 | MODERATE |
| Adult eunuch | 50 | MODERATE |

Premium for eunuchs: **150%** — reflecting anticipated career value [MODERATE confidence].

### C. Population Estimates

| Period | Population | Confidence |
|--------|-----------|------------|
| Justinian's peak (541) | 25-30 million | MODERATE |
| Post-7th century losses | ~10-12 million | MODERATE |
| 10th century recovery | ~12-18 million | MODERATE |
| After 1204 | 2-4 million | MODERATE |
| 1453 | ~500,000 or less | MODERATE |

Constantinople: **300,000-500,000** at peak → **~70,000** during Dark Ages → **~50,000** at 1453 fall.

### D. Literacy Estimates

Average: **~30%** (Brownworth estimate), though "plunged well below 30%" during 7th-8th century crisis [LOW confidence].

### E. Theme System

- Themes expanded: **4** (640s-660s) → **29** (10th c.) → **38** (11th c.)
- Total thematic soldiers: **~120,000** circa 775; **100,000-120,000** around 900 CE [MODERATE confidence]

### F. Gini Estimate

Byzantine 1000 CE: **Gini 0.40-0.45** with **highest top 1% income share (30.6%)** in Milanovic's pre-modern sample [MODERATE confidence].

## III. TANG DYNASTY CHINA (618-907 CE)

### A. Aristocratic Clan Structure

Nicolas Tackett's database: ~**30,000 individuals** reconstructed into ~**4,000 patrilines** from **3,329+ tomb epitaphs**. Analysis reveals **87 core patrilines** representing **60% of known elite individuals**, connected through **384 documented marriages** [HIGH confidence].

### B. Examination System Metrics (BENCHMARK DATA)

| Metric | Value | Confidence |
|--------|-------|------------|
| Total jinshi awarded (entire Tang) | 6,585 degrees | HIGH |
| Average jinshi per year | ~23 | HIGH |
| Candidates per year (jinshi) | 1,000-2,000 | HIGH |
| Jinshi pass rate | **1-2%** | HIGH |
| Mingjing pass rate | 10-20% | HIGH |

**Chancellor backgrounds over time:**
- Emperor Xuanzong (713-756): ~33% examination background
- Emperor Xianzong (806-821): ~60% examination background  
- 9th century overall: **~85%** examination background

A 2024 PNAS study confirms aristocratic pedigree's effect **declined throughout Tang** while examination success **increased in importance** [HIGH confidence].

### C. Land Allocation (Equal-Field System)

| Category | Allocation |
|----------|------------|
| Adult male (ding) | **100 mu total** |
| — Koufen tian (returnable) | 80 mu |
| — Yongye tian (heritable) | 20 mu |
| Infirm/disabled | 40 mu |
| Widows/concubines | 30 mu |

System collapsed by ~705; formally replaced by Two-Tax System in **780 CE** [HIGH confidence].

### D. Gini Estimate

**NO SCHOLARLY GINI ESTIMATE EXISTS FOR TANG CHINA** — major data gap.

## IV. ABBASID CALIPHATE (750-1258 CE)

### A. Ethnic Composition Transformation

Under Umayyads: mawali (non-Arab Muslim converts) faced systematic discrimination. Abbasids abolished formal mawali discrimination, opening administrative positions to Persian bureaucratic class.

### B. Bulliet Conversion Curve Estimates

| Region | ~750 CE | ~800 CE | ~900 CE | ~1000 CE | Confidence |
|--------|---------|---------|---------|----------|------------|
| Iran | 7% | 45% | Majority | ~80% | MODERATE |
| Iraq | — | ~40% | — | — | LOW |
| Egypt | — | — | ~50% | — | LOW |

### C. Turkish Military Elite

Al-Mu'tasim (r. 833-842): assembled **at least 7,000 Turkic slave soldiers** (ghulam) — force large enough to cause Baghdad riots and necessitate moving capital to Samarra in 836 [HIGH confidence].

Mamluk price: **"three to four times that of a top warhorse"** [MODERATE confidence].

### D. Population Estimates

Baghdad peak (9th century): **800,000-1,200,000** inhabitants — likely world's largest city until 930s [MODERATE confidence].

Slave population of Iraq (late 9th century): ~**300,000**; Basra area: ~**15,000 slaves** representing ~**25% of labor force** [MODERATE confidence].

### E. Gini Estimate

**NO SCHOLARLY GINI ESTIMATE EXISTS FOR ABBASID CALIPHATE** — major data gap.

## V. OTTOMAN EMPIRE (1299-1922 CE)

### A. Askeri-Reaya Division

- **Reaya** (tax-paying subjects): **>90%** of population
- **Askeri** (military-administrative, tax-exempt): **<10%** of population

[HIGH confidence]

### B. Devşirme System Quantification (CRITICAL DATA)

| Metric | Value | Confidence |
|--------|-------|------------|
| Total boys collected (15th-17th c.) | **200,000-300,000** | MODERATE |
| Collection frequency | Every 4-5 years | HIGH |
| Collection ratio | **1 boy per 40 households** | HIGH |
| Boys per levy | ~3,500 | MODERATE |
| Age range | 8-18 (typically 8-14) | HIGH |
| Grand Viziers from Enderun | **64** | HIGH |

Post-1453: Grand Viziers chosen "nearly exclusively from the kul system" — men of "Byzantine or Balkan background" [HIGH confidence].

### C. Timar System

**37,818 timar holders** recorded in 1525 tax rolls [HIGH confidence].

| Classification | Annual Revenue (Akçe) |
|---------------|----------------------|
| Timar | Under 20,000 |
| Ziamet | 20,000-100,000 |
| Has | Over 100,000 |

Early timariot ethnic composition (1431 Albania): **16% Christian cavalrymen**, 30% Turks, 50% Sultan's slaves [HIGH confidence].

### D. Janissary Corps Evolution

| Period | Numbers | Confidence |
|--------|---------|------------|
| 1389 (Kosovo) | 2,000 | MODERATE |

### E. Religious Demographics

- Muslims: **~two-thirds** of empire population
- Turks: **~40%** — minority in their own empire
- Istanbul 16th c.: ~60% Muslim, ~30% Christian, ~10% Jewish
- Jewish population: **150,000** (world's largest by late 16th c.)
- Slave population (Constantinople 16th-17th c.): **~20%**

[MODERATE confidence]

### F. Guild Structure (Istanbul)

Per Evliya Çelebi (17th c.): **158 artisan associations** with **79,764 esnaf members** operating from **23,214 ateliers and shops** [HIGH confidence].

### G. Gini Estimate

Ottoman cities (18th c.): **Gini 0.70+** (wealth-based) [MODERATE confidence — Coşgel & Ergene 2012].

## VI. VENETIAN REPUBLIC (697-1797 CE)

### A. Libro d'Oro and Patrician Tracking (BEST DOCUMENTED)

Libro d'Oro established **1315**, maintained until 1797 — near-complete elite registry for 482 years.

| Category | Count | Timeframe |
|----------|-------|-----------|
| Case Vecchie ("Old Houses") | 24-25 families | Pre-800 CE |
| Case Nuove ("New Houses") | ~150 families | 800-1297 CE |
| Case Nuovissime | 30 families | After 1381 |
| Case Fatte Per Soldo (Candia) | ~75 families | 1645-1669 |
| Case Fatte Per Soldo (Morea) | ~50 families | 1684-1718 |

### B. Great Council Membership Time Series

| Year | Members | Notes |
|------|---------|-------|
| 1143 | 35 | Counsellors |
| 1297 | ~950 | After Serrata |
| 1340 | 1,212 | — |
| 1527 | **2,746** | Maximum |
| Post-1630 plague | 1,660 | — |
| 1797 | ~1,100 | Final |

[HIGH confidence]

### C. Elite Closure Metrics

- Pre-Serrata annual family exit rate: **1.2%**
- Post-Serrata annual family exit rate: **0.14%**
- Reduction: **Order of magnitude**

Between 1297-1381: only **15 families** added in 84 years [HIGH confidence].

### D. Three-Tier Structure

| Class | Percentage | Functions |
|-------|------------|-----------|
| Nobili (Patricians) | 5-7% | Government |
| Cittadini (Citizens) | 7-8% | Bureaucracy |
| Popolani (Commoners) | 85-90% | Labor |

### E. Gini Estimates (HIGHEST QUALITY DATA)

| Community Type | Average Gini |
|---------------|--------------|
| Cities | ~0.90 |
| Agro-towns | ~0.85 |
| Rural communities | ~0.79 |

Bergamo specifically: **Gini 0.715-0.753** (16th-17th c.) [HIGH confidence — Alfani research].

**Venetian taxation was regressive**: poorest 10% taxed **5.4-6% of income** while richest 5% paid only **3.9-4.4%** (1550) [HIGH confidence].

### F. Doge Family Concentration

**118 doges** from only **59 families**. Top 8 families produced **43 doges (36%)** [HIGH confidence].

## VII. MONGOL EMPIRE (1206-1368 CE)

### A. Documentation Limitations

Principal sources: Rashīd al-Dīn's *Jāmiʿ al-Tawārīkh* (c. 1307), *Secret History of the Mongols*, Yuan Chinese records. Empire's unified period: only **54 years** (1206-1260).

### B. Chinggisid Lineage

Only Chinggisids from legitimate line could rule under the "Chinggisid Principle" — persisted until 19th century.

Modern Kazakh Tore clan: ~**8,000 verified descendants** through documented genealogies [MODERATE confidence].

### C. Keshig (Imperial Guard)

| Period | Size | Confidence |
|--------|------|------------|
| 1203 (founding) | 150 | HIGH |
| Early Genghis | 1,000 | HIGH |
| By 1206 quriltai | **10,000** | HIGH |
| Kublai Khan | 12,000 | MODERATE |

Keshig served as primary elite formation pathway [HIGH confidence].

### D. Yuan Four-Class System

| Class | Composition | Est. % | Confidence |
|-------|-------------|--------|------------|
| Mongols | Ruling ethnicity | ~1.5% | MODERATE |
| Semu | Central/West Asians | ~1.5% | MODERATE |
| Hanren | Former Jin subjects | ~14% | MODERATE |
| Nanren | Former Song subjects | **~83%** | MODERATE |

1290 Yuan census: ~**10 million Hanren** + ~**60 million Nanren** = ~**58 million taxed** [MODERATE confidence].

### E. Examination Restoration (1315)

Equal quotas: **75 provincial + 25 metropolitan per class = 300 total jinshi**, though difficulty intentionally lower for Mongols and Semu [HIGH confidence].

### F. Fundamental Demographic Reality

Mongol homeland: ~**800,000** people. Empire at peak: **>100 million** inhabitants. Yuan China: Mongols = **~1.5%** of population — ratio of **~100:1** ruled to rulers [MODERATE confidence].

### G. Gini Estimate

**NO SCHOLARLY GINI ESTIMATE EXISTS FOR MONGOL EMPIRE** — major data gap.
